# 👃 Nose Fold - Viral Paper Face Fold Illusion

Recreate the viral "paper nose folding" illusion right in your browser! Upload or take a photo, draw two squiggly lines across the nose bridge, and collapse the space in-between for hilarious squished faces.

## ✨ Features

- **📱 Precision Aiming Loupe**: A 2.2x magnifying glass floats ~85px above your finger so you can aim with pixel precision on mobile touchscreens without your finger blocking the view.
- **✂️ Photo Framing & Cropping**: Touch crop box with 1:1, 4:5, 3:4, and Free aspect ratios, plus 90° rotation for phone photos.
- **⚡ Instant Before / After**: Tap anywhere on the face or use the tactile toggle button (supports tap or hold-to-peek).
- **🎞️ Transition Animated GIF (.gif)**: Export smooth looping fold/unfold animations directly in pure JavaScript.
- **🔥 Before & After Snap GIF (.gif)**: Rapid 2-frame flip meme GIF with high-contrast "BEFORE" and "AFTER" corner badges.
- **📸 High-Res PNG & Side-by-Side Split**: 1-click snapshot downloads and split comparison photos.
- **📺 Fullscreen Presentation Mode**: Immersive mode that turns your phone into an interactive flapping paper face toy.
- **🔊 Sound & Haptics**: Synthesized paper rustle/squash sounds and tactile vibration on mobile devices.
- **🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy**: All processing, folding, and GIF encoding runs locally in your browser. No photos are ever uploaded to an external server.

## 🚀 Built With

- [React 19](https://react.dev/) + [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
- [Vite](https://vite.dev/)
- [TailwindCSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- [gifenc](https://github.com/mattdesl/gifenc) (Fast pure-JS GIF encoder)
